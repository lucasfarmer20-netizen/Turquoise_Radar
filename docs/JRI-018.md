# JRI-018 — compact image-first sourcing feed

The sourcing feed now presents large private item photographs, dated observed
prices, supported closing times, a short price assessment, **GO / NO GO** and a
direct marketplace link. **Research** opens the existing full evidence view:
acquisition components, Alaska shipping, seller claims, photographs, source/query
provenance, original observations and owner review history remain there.

This change builds on JRI-017 PR #29, which depends on JRI-016 #28 and JRI-015 #27.
Review and merge #27 first, retarget/revalidate #28, then #29 and this change in
order. No receiver infrastructure or JRI-014 closeout work is included.

## Scanning and photographs

The responsive grid fits four columns on a wide desktop and one column on a
phone. Cards use the same compact layout for observed bids, minimum bids and
historical realized prices. Full research paragraphs and financial tables are
absent from the card face. The scope notice stays available in an expandable
**NEW-ONLY · incomplete history** header.

The primary **Open** view includes only surviving listings with verified local
item photographs. **Needs Photos** retains open items lacking usable photographs;
**Ended** retains ended opportunities, and **Reviewed** exposes passed or
purchased records. These filters never delete inventory or erase research.
Viewing an image opens its larger private copy. No remote images, email resources
or marketplace pages are fetched to render the feed.

Photo selection checks the existing owner/case/listing association, active status
and stored image integrity. Description and shipping-document attachments do not
qualify as item photos. Each private thumbnail/full-image request rechecks that
the listing remains eligible and unsuppressed; stale image URLs cannot bypass
deletion safeguards. Missing or corrupt files put the item in Needs Photos.

## Conservative price and time labels

| Assessment | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| POTENTIAL DEAL | Supported resale or verified net-metal economics meet the configured acquisition target. |
| FAIR PRICE | Supported value covers acquisition costs, but the target margin is not established. |
| TOO EXPENSIVE | Complete acquisition costs exceed supported break-even value. |
| NEEDS INFO | A trustworthy price assessment is blocked by missing, stale or conflicting evidence, or the auction has ended. |

An economic assessment requires confirmed USD acquisition components, confirmed
Alaska fulfillment, reviewed condition/material evidence and an observed quote
dated on the current Alaska calendar day. Unknown costs remain unknown. A newer
displayed price cannot reuse an older, cheaper financial calculation. Conflicting
quotes or displayed-versus-analyzed cost differences require more information.

Resale support must come from retained comparable records matching the confirmed
range and dataset; a manually typed resale scenario alone is insufficient. Melt
support requires verified eligible net-silver weight, verified sterling and
spot evidence with an explicit, nonfuture timestamp on the current Alaska date.
A gross seller weight, a hallmark on one component
or seller-reported testing does not authenticate the entire lot. These compact
assessment gates leave the underlying financial calculation and saved evidence
unchanged.

Prices say **Last observed** with the supported observation date; source email
dates are used instead of import timestamps. Date-only owner observations retain
their exact-time uncertainty. Ended auction results say **Realized**, not current
bid or owner purchase cost. A low starting bid cannot by itself earn a deal label.
Unknown currency displays the numeric amount without an inferred currency symbol.

Countdowns require a lot-specific deadline with an explicit timezone. They show
the remaining interval to the recorded close, **ENDED** after that deadline, or
an unknown closing time. The countdown updates locally without requests and
does not assert current marketplace availability. Exact recorded deadlines are
available on hover and in full research.

## Owner feedback

**GO** saves the existing **Interested** action and moves the review state to
WATCH. **NO GO** saves **Passed** and moves it to PASSED. The card immediately
reflects a successful save, and the state survives a reload. The other button or
existing review workflow can reverse the choice; earlier Feedback rows remain.
Neither action bids, buys, verifies material or confirms acquisition profitability.
NEEDS INFO never forces an owner decision.

The existing promotion path handles an unpromoted candidate within the same
transaction as its explicit feedback. Repeated submissions of the same current
decision are idempotent; conflicting stale decisions request a reload. Search
feedback milestones retain their existing once-per-listing semantics. Purchased
records require the workbench workflow instead of these sourcing shortcuts.

task makes no new marketplace requests and authorizes no purchasing, bidding,
recurring intake or new infrastructure.
