## Pictorial descriptions are not maker names

The pilot exposed a source caption, “arrow head,” appearing in the named-maker
candidate list. The directory stores both source names and description-only
captions in `maker_name`; `identity_kind=UNRESOLVED` also occurs on genuine named
makers, so excluding every such record would remove useful single-source leads.

Ranking policy `jri006-owner-likely-maker-v2` separates labels made entirely from
pictorial-description vocabulary and explicit unidentified labels into **Related
marks with UNKNOWN maker**. Their source references and original labels remain
available, and they do not consume any of the five named-candidate positions.
Full names such as “Bear: Rhoda Jack,” “Sunburst Handcrafts Inc.” and “Arrowhead
Trading Post” remain eligible. Source count and independent corroboration are not
additional gates. This is a conservative description check, not a general person
name recognizer or authentication system; other malformed captions still require
source review.

Existing saved searches are preserved. The UI applies an explicitly labeled display
correction to cached description-only candidates without updating their stored
JSON. Such labels cannot be confirmed as makers, reintroduced as maker corrections,
or used to calculate maker-premium scenarios. Historical owner entries remain in
history; a description-only confirmation does not establish an accepted maker.
New searches record the new policy version. Reference records, source rights and
the frozen evaluation/index are unchanged.
