### Private triage and evidence-separated reviews

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m radar.references.cli triage --source SOURCE_ID --limit 50
.venv/bin/python -m radar.references.cli review-item data/private-reference-review.json
.venv/bin/python -m radar.references.cli review-report
```

These commands make no network requests. Triage flags catalog wording for Bell,
Southwestern/Native American, turquoise cuff, White Buffalo, Mexican/Taxco,
alpaca/German silver, imported origin, imitation claims, plating and base metals.
Import origin and imitation are separate topics. Mexican origin alone never creates
an import, imitation or base-metal label. Copper wording may flag a candidate,
including a legitimate craft object; it is not a counterfeit determination.

`ReferenceReview` uses `schema_version=jri-review-1`, an item ID, reviewer, date,
KEEP/HOLD/REJECT decision, image quality, reviewed photo/crop IDs, rationale and
findings. Each finding contains a topic, literal value and basis:

- `SOURCE_CLAIM`: retained catalog URL or same-item assertion; gallery attribution
  remains a claim, including a named maker, listed weight or reported composition.
- `PHOTO_OBSERVATION`: same-item reviewed photo/crop IDs; record what is visible,
  such as a partial inscription or construction feature, without inferring the
  object's authorship, cultural identity, composition or authenticity.
- `INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE`: same-item DOCUMENTED assertions from owner inspection,
  owner-reported tests or independent tests, with the original evidence/test scope.
  A gallery/museum attribution cannot be relabeled as independent evidence.

Reviews are retained as private append-only `REFERENCE_REVIEW` assertions. A new
decision supersedes the prior review; repeated identical submissions are idempotent.
Reports count the latest eligible review once per object, separating finding topic
and basis, decision and image quality. Excluded/removed images invalidate the review
count. Reviews never set authentication, financial evidence or training readiness.

Record imported/plated/base-metal cases as research candidates until their actual
evidence is reviewed. A seller's claim, hallmark match or geographic origin alone
does not establish a genuine negative, counterfeit or whole-object composition.
Independent tests need method and sampled scope; do not generalize a local result.

`related_item_ids` records an explicit conservative split relationship. All views
and crops already share the stable source/item identity. Related-item components
receive a shared split-group assertion, including transitive relationships through
an excluded intermediate item. Thus related endpoints remain together even in
item-mode exports. Grouping does not authenticate a maker or prove physical identity;
relationships remain conservative and are not automatically unlinked by a review
correction. Exact/near duplicate and artist grouping continue to apply.
