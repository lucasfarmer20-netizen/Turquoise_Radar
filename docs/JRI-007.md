Inbox, source identities and alert observations. ShopGoodwill precedes HiBid,
then CTBids and the estate directories at equal precedence. Within a source
priority, specific overlooked-listing cues come first, then generic jewelry
vocabulary and the existing tag count. Ties retain their incoming order.
This is not a profitability score. Filter for
misspelled turquoise, Southwestern vocabulary, poorly described pieces, silver
or mixed lots. Other sources remain in the full inbox and Quick Capture.

Each card shows the observed price, evidenced lot closing, seller/auction IDs,
six separately labelled cost components and their estimated/confirmed/unknown
status. Missing premiums, service fees, handling, tax and Alaska delivery stay
unknown. **Quoted shipping** carries only the amount's evidence status. The
separate destination display retains CONFIRMED_TO_ALASKA, CONFIRMED_UNAVAILABLE
or UNKNOWN. For example, a $12 CONFIRMED quote can coexist with UNKNOWN or
unavailable Alaska fulfillment. An auction-wide start-of-closing quote is not a
lot deadline. Generic shipping availability or a quoted amount does not establish
Soldotna delivery.

Choose **Review candidate**, then **Send to purchase-review queue**. The existing
workbench retains fee profiles, Decimal bid calculations and comparable-sale
evidence requirements. A linked candidate now offers **Research likely maker**,
opening `/identify` with that listing. No purchase, bid, maker acceptance, valuation
or source-query credit is implied by these actions.
