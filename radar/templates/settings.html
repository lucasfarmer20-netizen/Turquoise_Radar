OWNER PREFERENCES

Make the assumptions explicit.

Rates are editable planning assumptions, not current marketplace fee claims. Resale target bands do not create listing estimates. Decimal amounts use strings; rates use fractions (0.60 = 60%).

Silver has no default price. Enter spot_usd_per_troy_oz together with spot_timestamp (ISO 8601) and spot_source. The demo seed uses an explicitly synthetic spot scenario.

Buyer profiles calculate premium on item price, service rate on item price plus a per-lot fee, then tax on item + premium + service. Confirm each marketplace’s taxable base; enter observed absolute tax where necessary. Observed explicit fees override profiles.
